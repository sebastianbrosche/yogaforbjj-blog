🧘 Mobility Drill
90/90 Hip Stretch
  1. Sit on the floor with one leg bent 90° in front and the other 90° behind.
  2. Keep your spine tall — don't collapse forward.
  3. Gently lean toward the front leg until you feel a stretch in the back hip.
  4. Breathe deeply for 5 breaths, then switch sides.
⏱ 2 min per side — do daily
