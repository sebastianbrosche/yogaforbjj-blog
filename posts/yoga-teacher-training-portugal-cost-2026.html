How Much Does Yoga Teacher Training Cost in Portugal in 2026? A Complete Breakdown

By Sebastian Brosche 2026-06-10 · 9 min read

When I decided to do my 200-hour yoga teacher training in Portugal, I had no idea what it would actually cost. Every website said "contact us for pricing." Nobody would give me a straight answer. So I'm going to give you one: here's exactly what you'll spend, where the money goes, and how to budget without surprises.

The Total Cost Range for YTT in Portugal

For a 200-hour yoga teacher training in Portugal, expect to spend between €2,500 and €5,000 total. This includes tuition, accommodation, food, flights, and incidentals. The wide range depends on location, program intensity, accommodation type, and time of year.

Tuition Costs

Tuition for 200-hour YTT programs in Portugal typically ranges from €1,800 to €3,500. Programs in the Algarve (where Heat Lagos runs its trainings) tend to be at the higher end due to the location, facilities, and quality of instruction.

At Heat Lagos, our 200-hour Vinyasa Yoga Teacher Training is competitively priced and includes access to our infrared-heated studio, small class sizes (max 12 students), and instruction from teachers with 15+ years of experience.

Accommodation Options

Lagos has accommodation ranging from shared hostels (€25-40/night) to private Airbnbs (€60-120/night). For a 15-day training, expect:

  • Shared apartment or hostel: €375-600 total
  • Private room Airbnb: €900-1,800 total
  • Mid-range hotel: €1,200-2,400 total

Flights to Portugal

Flights to Faro Airport (the closest airport to Lagos) vary by origin and season. From Europe: €50-200 round trip. From North America: €400-800 round trip. From Asia/Australia: €600-1,200 round trip.

Food and Daily Expenses

Lagos is affordable for food. Budget €15-25 per day for meals if eating out, or €8-12 per day if cooking at your accommodation. Total for 15 days: €120-375.
