The Daily BJJ Mobility Routine: 15 Minutes That Will Change Your Jiu-Jitsu

June 2026

Why Daily Mobility Matters

Mobility is not something you gain once and keep forever. It decays. Every day you sit at a desk, drive to the gym, and train in a sport that compresses your joints, your mobility decreases a little.

The only way to maintain the range of motion your BJJ needs is daily maintenance. Not once a week. Not when you feel stiff. Daily.

This 15-minute routine covers the six BJJ critical areas: hips, spine, shoulders, neck, ankles, and wrists. Do it every day — on training days as part of your warm-up, on rest days as a standalone practice.

The 15-Minute Routine

Minute 0-3: Spine and Hips

  • Cat-cow — 10 slow breaths (wakes up the spine)
  • Child's pose with hip circles — 60 seconds (opens hips and lower back)
  • Deep squat hold — 60 seconds (hips, ankles, and spinal position)

Minute 3-6: Hip Mobility

  • 90-90 stretch — 90 seconds each side (external/internal rotation)
  • Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch — 60 seconds each side
  • Figure-four glute stretch — 60 seconds each side

Minute 6-9: Shoulders and Upper Back

  • Thread the needle — 60 seconds each side (thoracic rotation)
  • Band pull-aparts or clasped hands overhead — 60 seconds
  • Eagle arms — 60 seconds each side (shoulder external rotation)

Minute 9-11: Neck and Wrists

  • Chin tucks — 10 reps (deep neck flexor activation)
  • Neck rotations — 5 each direction
  • Wrist flexion/extension — 30 seconds each

Minute 11-13: Active Movement

  • Walking lunges with rotation — 8 reps each side
  • World's greatest stretch — 60 seconds each side

Minute 13-15: Breath and Reset

  • Constructive rest — 2 minutes (breathe into your ribs)

Make It Automatic

The hardest part is not the routine. It is doing it every day. Attach it to an existing habit: do it right after your morning shower, or right before you leave for the gym. The consistency is what creates results.

Fifteen minutes a day. That is all it takes to feel better on the mats.
